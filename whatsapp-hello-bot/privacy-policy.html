Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the School Hello Bot WhatsApp assistant (“the Service”) handles information when people message the bot.

Who this is for

The Service is a simple WhatsApp greeting/FAQ assistant intended for school event organizers and parents. It is operated for development and pilot use.

Information we process

  • WhatsApp phone number (as provided by Meta WhatsApp Cloud API)
  • Message content you send to the bot
  • Basic technical delivery metadata needed to send replies

We do not ask parents to create accounts. We do not intentionally collect student names, school IDs, or payment information in this early version.

How we use information

  • To receive your WhatsApp message and send a reply
  • To operate, debug, and improve the Service
  • To keep the Service secure and prevent abuse

How messages are delivered

Messages are transmitted through Meta’s WhatsApp Cloud API. Our application server processes the webhook event and may send a response back through Meta.

Data retention

We aim to keep as little data as possible. Message content may be stored temporarily in logs for troubleshooting and then deleted. Do not send sensitive personal information to the bot.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers needed to run the Service (for example hosting, Meta WhatsApp, and logging tools).

Children’s privacy

The Service is intended for parents and organizers. It is not directed at children under 13. Please do not submit children’s personal information.

Your choices

You can stop using the bot at any time by not messaging it. To request deletion of retained logs related to your number, contact the operator below.

Contact

For privacy questions about this pilot Service, contact the app operator through the Meta developer / business account that owns this app.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
