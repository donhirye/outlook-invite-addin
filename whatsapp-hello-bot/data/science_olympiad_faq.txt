Demo materials for invited testers. Untagged answers may be parent-reported from the 6th Graders WhatsApp group; they are not official school verification. Answers marked [DEMO ANSWER] include invented demo details.

## Lost & Found

Q: Where is Lost & Found at Crone?
A: Crone has two Lost & Found locations: one in the cafeteria/lunchroom and another outside the gym. Students should check both locations.

Q: Can parents enter the school to collect lost items?
A: Students are generally expected to retrieve their own belongings from Lost & Found. If you need to make a special arrangement, contact the school office.

Q: What if my child cannot find an item in Lost & Found?
A: Ask your child to check both Lost & Found locations and then check with the school office. If the item hasn't been turned in yet, it may help to check again later.

Q: What if my child leaves something on the school bus?
A: Contact First Student with your child's bus number and route details. Parents have reported that drivers sometimes retain found belongings on the bus until the following morning.

Q: How much does a replacement student ID cost?
A: Parents reported that replacement student IDs are available through the school office for $5.

Q: Can my child buy lunch without a student ID?
A: Parents reported that students can still purchase school lunch without physically presenting their student ID. Your child can ask cafeteria staff for assistance.

Q: Can the bot tell me whether a lost item has been found?
A: No. The bot does not have access to the school's live Lost & Found inventory. Your child should check the Lost & Found locations or contact the school office.

Q: How do I report a missing Chromebook?
A: Ask your child to notify their teacher and the school office. The school technology team will open a missing-device report and provide an update within two school days. [DEMO ANSWER]

Q: Can I get a replacement PE uniform?
A: Replacement PE uniforms can be purchased through the school office for $15 per set. Ask your child to confirm the available sizes before purchasing. [DEMO ANSWER]

Q: What happens to unclaimed Lost & Found items?
A: Unclaimed items are collected at the end of each grading quarter. Families receive a reminder before remaining clothing is donated. [DEMO ANSWER]
