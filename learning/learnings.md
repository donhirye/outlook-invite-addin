whatsapp.py takes:

- `to_phone` — who to message
- `body` — what text to send

and POSTs that to Meta’s WhatsApp Cloud API (using the tokens from `config.py`).

---

Rule of thumb: OOP isn’t required for FastAPI/LLM apps. Use classes when you have state + behavior that travel together, or multiple implementations of the same role — not just because “OOP is proper.”
